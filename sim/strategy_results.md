Two AETHER of external sponsorship is spent at 0.2 per round for ten rounds. All machines are evenly populated and every ticket is revealed.

Old claims remain escrowed independently of these future rounds (covered by Foundry multi-round tests). Zero demand produces zero new entry burn and zero new entry rewards. There is no APY promise.

10,000 seeded randomized rounds passed conservation and wallet-splitting checks. This model omits gas, transaction censorship, price, liquidity, sponsor motives, collusion and endogenous participation; it cannot establish market sustainability or appreciation.
